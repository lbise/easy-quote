# Easy Quote

Easy Quote helps very small artisan businesses prepare customer-ready quotes with less administrative work.

## Language

**Artisan Business**:
A sole artisan or very small company that performs skilled trade work for customers and prepares quotes for that work.
_Avoid_: Vendor, contractor, account

**Artisan**:
A person in an Artisan Business who understands the proposed work and supplies the quantities and materials needed for it.
_Avoid_: Operator, salesperson

**Customer**:
A reusable record of the person or organization for whom an Artisan Business proposes work.
_Avoid_: Client, buyer

**Quote**:
An editable commercial document describing proposed work for a Customer, including quantities and prices, that an Artisan reviews before sending.
_Avoid_: Estimate, invoice, proposal

**Quote PDF**:
The customer-ready PDF representation of a reviewed Quote.
_Avoid_: Printout, report

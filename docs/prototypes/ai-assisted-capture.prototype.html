PROTOTYPE · Throwaway logic, not production

When should capture ask, mark incomplete, or refuse?

This demo tests how spoken and written Artisan input becomes a reviewed Quote change. It asks one concise question only when the answer changes commercial meaning. Other missing facts remain visible as "needs Artisan input" markers for review.

Question under test: Can an Artisan get useful structure from AI-assisted capture without the system inventing commercial facts or turning drafting into an interview?

  • Speech uses explicit push-to-talk. Recording has a visible state and can be cancelled. This prototype keeps a transcript as input, never raw audio in the resulting draft.
  • Written and spoken input use the same extraction path. Extraction proposes changes. It never mutates a Quote without Artisan review and apply.
  • Each asserted fact shows evidence from Artisan input or an explicitly selected Customer, Business Default, Business Catalog, Customer Note, or Business Guidance.
  • AI may structure or reword evidence. It may not invent Customer identity, quantity, unit, material, Catalog Item match, price, VAT treatment, payment or acceptance terms, or validity.
  • A concise clarification is reserved for commercial forks: quantity or unit, priced scope versus option or exclusion, who performs or supplies, or which Customer or Catalog Item was meant.
  • Absent prices, Customer details, terms, and publication requirements create draft markers instead of more questions. The Artisan may defer a clarification into the same kind of marker.
  • Customer Notes, Business Guidance, and Catalog Items only enter a run when selected. Nothing is learned, saved, or updated by capture.

Should an optional catalog stay separate from Quote prices?

This click-through asks whether an Artisan can draft without a catalog, reuse one generic catalog-entry shape when it helps, and keep every Quote line and Quote Revision as an editable or preserved snapshot rather than a live catalog reference.

Proposed boundary to challenge: the Business Catalog is useful reusable content, not a pricing authority. Saving or updating catalog content always needs the Artisan's explicit acceptance. The two possible suggestion timings are visible below.

  • Quote creation works when the Business Catalog is empty or switched off.
  • One generic entry shape covers labor, materials, and fixed work. Trade words live in editable description, detail, category, and search terms.
  • An entry has description, optional detail, category, search terms, unit, pricing mode, and an optional default price. It has no VAT, currency, supplier account, or trade type.
  • CHF and VAT treatment belong to the Quote and Artisan Business, never to a catalog entry.
  • Adding an entry copies its content into a Quote line. Editing the Quote line never changes the catalog, and later catalog changes never rewrite existing lines or published Quote Revisions.
  • An ad-hoc confirmed line or a meaningful price override can create a visible save or update suggestion. Rejecting it changes nothing.
  • The Artisan may receive a suggestion right after confirming a line, or collect it for a quieter review. This prototype does not choose between those timings.
  • VAT, discounts, and levies are Quote calculation controls. They are not catalog entries disguised as work or materials.
  • Default prices are optional and are not evidence of a stable supplier price. The Artisan confirms every Quote price.

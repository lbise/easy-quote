What exactly is a Quote?

This demo tests whether one canonical Quote model can represent the artisan examples while making draft numbering, finalization, duplication, explicit revisions, totals, options, exclusions, estimates, and VAT behaviour predictable.

Proposed answer under test: a numbered Quote owns immutable revisions; each revision contains structured scope lines plus an explicit calculation; finalization freezes a revision, revision creates the next version of the same commercial offer, and duplication creates a new offer.

  • A Quote number is reserved when its first draft is created.
  • Only drafts can change; finalization stores an immutable snapshot.
  • A revision keeps the Quote number and increments its revision number.
  • A duplicate gets a new Quote number and starts at revision 1.
  • Price basis is explicit: fixed, approximate estimate, or time and materials.
  • Optional/excluded lines never silently enter the payable total.
  • Inclusion, certainty, work performer, and material supplier are independent line attributes.
  • Rich technical details and reference images belong to a scope line without changing its price shape.
  • Discounts and levies are named, ordered adjustments with an explicit pre- or post-VAT stage.
  • The MVP uses one currency and one VAT treatment/rate per Quote.
  • Finalized PDFs display Customer, project, business, and VAT snapshots captured at that moment.
  • Accepted, declined, expired, or cancelled outcomes name the finalized revision they concern.
  • Acceptance or cancellation ends the Quote; decline or expiry can reopen only through an explicit revision.
